Industrial AI Lab

I am a doctoral researcher in mechanical engineering at Nanyang Technological University Singapore, working in the Industrial AI Lab (IAI) within the Robotics Research Centre (RRC), supervised by Prof. Marvin Carl May. I develop AI methods that connect engineering knowledge with decisions in manufacturing and supply chains.

I completed a double-degree master's program in mechanical engineering at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) and Tongji University, with research exchanges at MIT and NTU. At BMW Group in Munich, I worked on AI-supported digital cost engineering and supply chain decision support.

Research Interests

Smart ManufacturingMachine learning (ML) and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for industrial intelligence and knowledge-grounded decision support.
Manufacturing SystemsGraph learning, reinforcement learning (RL), and simulation for planning, scheduling, and adaptive manufacturing systems.
Humanoid-Enabled ManufacturingEmbodied AI, large language models (LLMs), and RL for humanoid task planning and human-robot collaboration.
AI for Production Network and Supply Chain DecisionsAI, optimization, and simulation for production network design, supply-chain resilience, and operational decisions.
